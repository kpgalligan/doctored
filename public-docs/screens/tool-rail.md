The tool rail, a narrow column of thirteen tool slots with the colour swatches at its foot. From
the top: the selection slot, which shows the lasso of the Polygonal Lasso because that was the
member last used, then Crop, Move, Brush, the healing slot, the toning slot, Fill, Gradient, the
shapes, the Pen, Text, the Eyedropper slot and Zoom. A small triangle in the lower right corner
marks each slot that holds more than one tool. The Brush slot is the chosen one, with a tinted fill
and a blue ring. Below an empty stretch, the black foreground swatch overlaps the white background
swatch, with the swap arrows at its upper right and the small default-colours squares at its lower
left.
