# Select ▸ Focus Area

Status: decided 2026-11-14 (batch c12-b2, [#17](https://github.com/kpgalligan/doctored/issues/17), which stays open for the Magnetic Lasso's part B); the colour modes of decision 7 added the same day (batch c13-c1). Kernel `core/src/focus_area.rs`, export `dr_selection_focus_area` (`core/src/ffi_focus_area.rs`), app `FocusArea.swift`, MCP `select_focus_area`.

Focus Area selects the part of a photograph that is in focus. The rule is Doctored's own; it is not measured against any other editor's result.

## Decisions

1. **Input.** The selection capture's 8-bit analysis source (`SelectionCapture.source`, `ImageDocument.analysisComposite`), the picture the Vision selections read: the composite at 8 bits, its display derivative at 16, the document's own tone-mapped display at 32-bit float (exposure 0, never the window's exposure or a candidate map). The output is byte coverage. No model and no download.
2. **Measure.** Stack Images' two-band sharpness score over Rec. 709 luma (`focus_stack::sharpness`): local detail energy at the fine and the coarse band, summed, 0 where the picture is transparent. One score, so Focus Area and Stack Images can never disagree on what is sharp.
3. **Floor.** Detail energy below `(1/255)²` (one 8-bit code RMS) is no detail. A flat area can be in focus or not and nothing in it says which, so the measure never selects it; the region rule (5) may.
4. **Threshold.** `range` 0–100 places the cut that fraction of the way up `ln(score)`, from `ln(floor)` to the picture's largest; a lower `range` selects more. Auto (no `range`) is Otsu's cut over a 256-bin histogram of the opaque pixels above the floor, reported as `range = 100·(k + 1)/256`. That value is a dyadic fraction, so it maps back onto the same cut and a recorded step replays bit for bit. When Otsu's two classes' mean `ln(score)` differ by less than `ln 4` (a fourfold energy ratio, twice the RMS detail), the picture has one focus and Auto takes range 0. A picture with no pixel above the floor selects nothing, and Auto reports 100.
5. **Regions.** One majority vote over Stack Images' vote box (twice the coarse band's radius: 8 px below a 4000-pixel side): a pixel is selected when more than half of the opaque pixels in its box are, and a tie keeps its own. Then every unselected opaque four-connected region that touches neither the canvas border nor a transparent pixel is filled (Quick Selection's hole fill, uncapped), so a sharp subject's smooth interior belongs to it. Coverage is the source alpha, so transparent pixels stay outside.
6. **Edge.** `soften_edge` (default true) feathers the coverage by the coarse band's radius (`gaussian_plane::feather`) and caps each pixel at its alpha.
7. **Scope.** Select ▸ Focus Area… and MCP `select_focus_area` answer where the analysis source is a picture of the document (`FocusArea.modes`; facts `.commonTool`, `.floatTool`, `.cmykTool`): RGB and Grayscale at 8, 16 and 32 bits (Grayscale R = G = B), Lab Color and CMYK at 8 and 16 (their Display P3 view, the composite the Vision selections read, not the L\*a\*b\* codes or the ink), and Indexed Color (the palette colours; a dithered conversion's texture is the document's own and reads as detail). Grayscale, Lab and Indexed list the tool and `showFocusArea:` in their closed sets; CMYK lists the selector and takes the tool by its `.cmykTool` fact. Two modes refuse. **Duotone:** its source is the gray channel through the inks' curves (`DuotoneDocument.display`), which need not be monotone, so a tone step in the channel can flatten or reverse in the view; it is refused for good with Select ▸ Subject and Sky (`DuotoneDocument.disabledTools`, [duotone-mode.md](duotone-mode.md)'s refused table). **Bitmap:** one bit holds no gradation, so a defocused edge and a sharp one are the same full-scale step and a dither scores as detail everywhere; Bitmap's Select menu offers no selection that reads the picture (Color Range, Similar and Subject are disabled), and the tool answers the mode's default sentence (`BitmapDocument.unoffered`). `scripts/verify_focus_area.py` runs every answering mode and both refusals; each mode's own drive calls `mode_check`.
8. **Dialog.** Auto (on by default), In-Focus Range (enabled when Auto is off) and Soften Edge. Apply sets the selection in the menu's Replace mode; there is no live preview, the selection is the preview and Undo takes it back. The recorded step carries the resolved `range`.
